# Deepstone privacy policy

**Last updated: October 29, 2026**
**Applies to the Deepstone Android app (com.youkar.deepstone, Google Play).**
**Publisher: Youkar.** Contact: YoukarC@hotmail.com

> This text describes what the app actually does. It is not legal advice.

Other languages: [Français](./) · [Nederlands](nl)

## In one sentence

Deepstone works without an account and without a server: your sessions and
your stones stay on your phone. The app does not use the internet.

## What stays on your device

Everything you create while using the app, stored locally in a database in the
app's private space:

- your focus sessions: start time, duration, chosen mine, status;
- the stones you obtain and the work you do on them;
- your settings;
- if you type one, a "reason for the session": an **optional** free-text note
  of 40 characters at most. It is copied onto the stone. It never appears in
  notifications or in the image of a stone that you share.

Uninstalling Deepstone removes this data from your device (see "Erasing your
data" for Android backup).

## No internet connection

The app has no permission to access the internet and contains no network code:
the app itself sends no data off your device.

## Permissions, and why

- **Notifications** — to tell you when a session ends, and to show a silent
  "session in progress" notification with the remaining time. Optional: the app
  works without them, and both can be turned off in settings.
- **Device startup** — to reschedule the end-of-session notification after the
  phone restarts.
- **Vibration** — haptic feedback, which can be turned off in settings.

Deepstone also uses the **accelerometer**: tilting the phone moves the light on
the stone. The reading is used live and is never recorded.

Deepstone asks for **no access** to your location, contacts, camera,
microphone or files.

## What the app does not do

There is **no account** to create. There is **no audience measurement**, no
analytics tool, **no advertising**, **no in-app purchases**, and no third-party
component that collects data. Nothing is sold, rented or passed on: there is no
mechanism to do so.

## Export, import, share

- **Export**: at your request, the app creates a JSON file with your sessions,
  stones and session reasons. It is **not encrypted**. You choose where it goes,
  through the system share menu. Once it leaves the app, the policy of the
  service you chose applies.
- **Import**: the app reads a file you select, through the system file picker.
- **Sharing a stone**: at your request, you send an image of the stone, which
  does not contain the session reason.

Nothing is sent automatically.

## Erasing your data

In the app: **Settings → erase everything** (immediate, permanent). Uninstalling
the app deletes its data from your device.

The app keeps Android's default system backup: if you have turned on Google
backup on your phone, Deepstone's data may be included (encrypted by Android)
and restored onto a new device. That backup is managed by Google, outside our
access. To delete it, use your phone's Google/Android settings.

## Children

Deepstone is for people aged 13 and over. It is not intended for children under
13 and does not knowingly collect any data about them (in any case, the app
collects no data).

## Your rights

The GDPR gives you the right of access, rectification, erasure and portability.
Here they work directly in the app: everything is visible there, the reason for
a session can be edited or cleared from the stone's page, export gives you a
file to take with you, and "erase everything" or uninstalling removes the data from your device. We
hold no copy of your data: we could neither give it to you nor delete it on
your behalf.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page is updated. Since the
app cannot contact you, this page is where to look.
